Beach Feedback

Help tune the walk ratings

What to send

The app gets the tide times from a nearby tide station. The bit that needs local help is the star rating: how much beach is actually usable at that place.

Send the beach, date, time, and your 0-5 rating. A short note or photo helps a lot.

Send it back to me in a message. If you have this app, you probably already know where to find me.

Star guide

0 starsNo beach. Do not bother.
1-2 starsNot really walkable. Too narrow, blocked, or closing fast.
3 starsGood enough for a proper walk. Worth going, but not loads of space.
4 starsGreat walk. Plenty of beach and room to enjoy it.
5 starsHuge beach. Big open walk, as far as you reasonably want to go.
